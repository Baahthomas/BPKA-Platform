About Kingdom Bearers Global

Kingdom Bearers Global is a vibrant and welcoming community dedicated to spiritual growth, fellowship, and service. We believe in fostering a supportive environment where individuals can explore their faith, build meaningful relationships, and make a positive impact in the world around them.

At Kingdom Bearers Global, we are more than a gathering—we are a movement of faith, hope, and love. Our heartbeat is to see lives transformed, families strengthened, and communities impacted through the power of God's Word and Spirit.

"Our mission as a church, is to declare the holistic message of balance with an ambidextrous mandate to incorporate earthly and spiritual principles for dominion."
